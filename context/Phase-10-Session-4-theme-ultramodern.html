Phase 10 · Session 4 · Visual direction fan-out

UltramodernTHEME 03 / 03

Soft, fluid, abstract. Surfaces behave like materials — frosted glass, machined edges, ambient light — but idealized past anything physical. Depth is real and earned: blur, layered translucency, glow. The register of present-day high-end consumer UI: Raycast's instrument, Vercel's typography, Apple's materials.

U5b

Stream · overlaid cards & Miller columns

The overlaid-cards metaphor taken literally: every card in the 7b stack becomes a pane of glass at a real altitude. Ancestors peek out from behind the next pane as slivers — you can see them through the pane in front. The tab layer becomes a row of floating pills; the active panel's marker becomes the gradient pill. Depth needs no edge lines at all here — altitude, blur, and light do everything.

Each ancestor is a real pane — dimmer and lower the further from focus — sliding out from behind the one in front at a tight 10px step (7b's left-step, become physical). The pill layer is now column-locked: each column's ancestry run starts exactly over its pane's left edge, so the pills map path → place at a glance. The gradient pill stays the active panel's marker. Where 7b fades border lines into the void, this theme fades whole panes by altitude — opacity, blur, shadow — the same depth ramp the token system would need to express.

D5 · Open questions
  • Blur is expensive: with dozens of panels and live SQLite subscriptions, does the material budget hold up, or does glass get rationed to overlays only?
  • Does "too perfect" fight hila's nature as a dense working tool — is this a marketing skin on a workshop?
  • Gradient accent vs the canon's single violet: is a two-stop accent still "monochrome + violet"?
  • Light mode glass needs a busy background to read as glass at all — is the ambient wash enough?
