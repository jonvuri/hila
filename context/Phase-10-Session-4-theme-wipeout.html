Phase 10 · Session 4 · Visual direction fan-out

Wipeout

THEME 01 / 03

Low-poly HUD language, rendered at modern fidelity. Typography is the chrome: micro-labels, flag bullets, and rules declare structure so boxes don't have to. Bounds are suggested — corner brackets, gutter ticks, a single chamfered corner — never drawn in full. Minimal and understated at rest; the accent is reserved for focus and live activity, so when something lights up, it means something.

01  flat fills · hard edges · no blur, no gradients, no shadows 02  monochrome + one accent = focus / selection / live 03  type suggests structure; chrome only suggests bounds 04  one corner cut per filled block, brackets on 3 corners max 05  depth = hard edge-steps, never soft elevation

W2c · Geometry rules
ONE CUT
Chamfer. Filled blocks get exactly one corner cut (8px). Two cuts is a shape; one cut is a mark.
Brackets. Max three corners; top-left is always reserved for the header. Full enclosure is the exception (menus, launcher).
1 · 2 · 4 · 8
Spacing. Powers of two (canon kept): 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 / 32 / 64. Marks are 5–8px; arms are 10px.
W3Chrome · panel anatomy & depth

A panel is: flag + title (top-left), a rule, brackets on the free corners. Focus changes color of the marks only — geometry never moves. Depth is a hard edge-step: ancestors compress to 1px edges with tab stubs (the overlaid-cards staircase, de-blurred and de-faded).

W5bStream · Overlaid cards & Miller columns

The 7b overlaid-cards mechanics, morphed: cards still overlay right-over-left with per-depth left/top steps and a universal tab layer (active panel's tab = the non-text marker, per Resolution B) — but the depth-fade masks become hard staircases. Tab baselines physically step down toward focus, edge lines brighten by depth, and nothing fades. The staircase is the HUD's z-axis. Five more concepts (depth gauge, edge-on sheets, spines, chevron flow, terrace) live in the dedicated deep dive: Wipeout × Overlaid Cards ▸

Log-structured engines append writes sequentially and compact later; page-oriented engines update in place.

The tab layer is the breadcrumb and the staircase: baselines step down 3px per depth toward focus, so the whole run reads as one descending instrument strip. Title tab = the inverted chamfered bar; ancestor tabs = outline bars (clickable, per 7b stage 1); non-active panel tab carries its title; the active panel's tab is the accent marker. Below, cards keep 7b's left/top offset mechanics — edge lines at 5px steps, tops at 3px steps — but where 7b fades edges into the void, Wipeout steps brightness: line-2 → line → ink-4 → accent at focus. No masks, no gradients.

D4 · Open questions (not dials — judgment calls)
  • Uppercase pressure: are mono micro-labels everywhere too shouty for an all-day tool, or exactly the discipline?
  • Does body text (user content) stay strictly untouched by the HUD voice — even counts/dates inside sentences?
  • The ghost-segment texture: charming instrument, or noise once real data is dense?
  • Danger hue in a monochrome world: hold-to-confirm strip enough, or does destructive need red at rest?
